PostCalendar

A personal posting calendar for my own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts.

PostCalendar is a small desktop tool that runs only on its owner's computer. It is used to:

  • track which of my accounts I have already posted to on each day;
  • upload videos to my own YouTube channels and add a comment from the channel, using the YouTube Data API;
  • connect my own Instagram professional accounts to show their usernames in the calendar.

It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts or servers of its own.
