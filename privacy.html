Privacy Policy

PostCalendar · Last updated: October 29, 2026

PostCalendar is a personal desktop tool used by its owner to manage posting to the owner's own YouTube channels and Instagram accounts. This policy explains what data it accesses and how it is handled.

Data the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google): the channel ID, title and handle of the channel you sign in with, and permission to upload videos and post comments on that channel.
  • Instagram (Meta): the account ID and username of the professional account you sign in with.
  • Videos and text you choose: the video file, title, description, tags and comment you enter for an upload.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show your accounts in the calendar, upload the videos you choose to your own channel, and post the comment you write. Nothing else is done with it.

Where the data is stored

All data, including sign-in tokens, is stored only on your own computer, in the app's folder. The app has no server, does not collect analytics and does not send your data to anyone other than Google (YouTube) and Meta (Instagram) when you use those features. Video files are deleted from the app's temporary folder after upload.

Sharing

Your data is not sold, shared or transferred to third parties.

Google API Services

PostCalendar uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy. PostCalendar's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke access at any time at Google account permissions and in Instagram under Settings → Apps and websites. Deleting the app's tokens folder removes all stored sign-in data from your computer.
